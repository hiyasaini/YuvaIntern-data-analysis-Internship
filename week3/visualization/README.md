# Week 3 – Visualizations

This folder contains the visualizations created for the Week 3 statistical analysis and hypothesis testing.

The plots help illustrate differences in tumor measurements, relationships between tumor radius and diagnosis, ANOVA group comparisons, and the 95% confidence interval.

### Visualizations Included

- `01_boxplot_mean_radius_by_diagnosis.png` – Compares mean tumor radius for benign and malignant tumors.
- `02_distribution_mean_radius_by_diagnosis.png` – Shows the distribution of tumor radius by diagnosis.
- `03_diagnosis_by_radius_category.png` – Shows diagnosis counts across radius categories.
- `04_confidence_interval_mean_radius_difference.png` – Shows the 95% confidence interval for the difference in mean tumor radius.
